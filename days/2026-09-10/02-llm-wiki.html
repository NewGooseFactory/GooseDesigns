Persistent is the risk word

Anything that accumulates can accumulate a mistake. A wiki that maintains itself has to be able to revise a page it wrote last week, and the description does not say how it decides to.

Interlinked is the useful half

An index gives you retrieval. Links between pages give you structure somebody can browse, which is the difference between a search box and a knowledge base.

What is not on this page

No file format, no link syntax, no update rule, no conflict handling. The trending description names the behaviour and not the mechanism.
