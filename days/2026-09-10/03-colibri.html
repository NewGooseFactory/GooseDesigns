Four claims, one mechanism

A big model fits if most of it stays on disk.

Frontier MoE on hardware you already own sounds impossible until you read the last clause. Mixture of experts activates a fraction of the network per token, so the rest can sit on disk and be paged in. The claim rests entirely on that.

The bottleneck moves, it does not vanish

Paging experts per token turns a memory problem into a read-latency problem. Whether that trade works depends on the disk, and no disk, throughput figure, or token rate appears in the description.
