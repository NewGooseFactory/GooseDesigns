Six words, three periods

Two verbs are for you. The third one is for someone else.

"Learn it. Build it. Ship it for others." Most teaching repositories stop after the second sentence. This one attaches a recipient to the last verb, which changes what finishing means.

The only sentence with a second party in it. Someone has to receive the thing, which is a harder bar than finishing it.

Why the third rung is longer

Learning and building are states you can declare. Shipping to someone is an event with a witness, so it fails in public when it fails.

From scratch

The repository name sets the starting point, and the three sentences set the finish line. Between them the whole curriculum is implied without a syllabus.
