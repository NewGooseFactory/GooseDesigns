Why a router

Agents don’t know whether to reach for jadx, apktool, Frida, IDA or BurpSuite on a given task. APK, ELF, JS, PCAP and CTF each need a different playbook.

Why one config

The routing core is driven by one structured configuration, validated by cross-platform CI and kept separate from optional client adapters.

Why a field journal

Because otherwise the same mistakes get repeated — the project’s own words for experience that never gets reused.
