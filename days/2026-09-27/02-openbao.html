Secrets, certificates and keys

A secret is not a stored string.
It is a lease.

OpenBao manages, stores and distributes sensitive data — and all secrets in OpenBao have a lease associated with them. At the end of the lease OpenBao automatically revokes the secret. Clients renew through built-in APIs, or they let it expire.

Why it exists

“Understanding who is accessing what secrets is already very difficult and platform-specific. Adding on key rolling, secure storage, and detailed audit logs is almost impossible without a custom solution.”

— project README
