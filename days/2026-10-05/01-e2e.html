End-to-end testing for web and mobile · Apache-2.0

Describe the goal once. Replay it without the model.

Describe a goal in natural language and an agent drives the app to reach it. When a later check verifies the step, its actions are recorded and the next run replays them with no model calls until the app changes. Tests without agent steps need no model at all.
