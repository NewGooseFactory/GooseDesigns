A job application framework built on Claude Code

The job search that runs on your machine.

Fill in your profile once. Claude evaluates postings, tailors your CV, writes cover letters and preps you for interviews, and a second agent reviews every draft before you see it.

Two ways to start, and the README is clear about which is which.

Running your own search? Use a private repository, with this one as upstream. git remote add upstream https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search.git Contributing changes back? Fork it. gh repo fork MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search --clone

Why it matters: a fork of a public repository is always public, and /setup writes your profile, from contact details to salary expectations, into tracked files.

The workflow

It drafts. You send.

The agent does the drafting and the checking; sending stays with you. At the core, a drafter and a reviewer work on the same application.

/setup

Builds your profile from your documents folder, a pasted CV, or an interview.

/scrape

Searches job portals, removes duplicates, and sorts matches by fit.

/apply <url>
  1. Evaluate fit
  2. →
  3. Draft CV and cover letter
  4. →
  5. Reviewer agent critiques
  6. →
  7. Revise
1
Postings are untrusted input.The workflow follows no instructions embedded in a posting and fetches no links from its body.
2
Follow-ups are drafts./outcome followup drafts a short note for quiet applications. It never sends, and offers at most two per application.
3
Gmail proposes; you approve./gmail-sync batches status changes for your approval and cites the source email on every one.
4
Hired is your call.Offers stop short of being marked hired or declined. And the defences are instructions, not a sandbox, so skim before you hit send.

Built in Denmark, made to travel. The portal skills target Danish job boards such as Jobindex, Jobnet and Akademikernes Jobbank. LinkedIn and freehire work across markets, and /add-portal generates a skill for a job board in yours.
