What happens before you see it

  1. Paths are checked against the real repository, and invalid output is retried with feedback.
  2. A deterministic compiler turns the validated graph into Mermaid, escaping all text.
  3. The browser sanitizes the source, renders in strict mode, and enforces the link allowlist again.
