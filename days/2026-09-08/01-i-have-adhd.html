Naming the failure beats promising the fix

Clear and concise are adjectives anybody can claim. Stop burying the answer describes a behaviour you can catch happening in a single response.

The title is the audience

Calling the repo i-have-adhd names who wrote it and who it is for. That is the same warranty as a byline, made from a first-person statement instead of a name.
