Tensors · dynamic neural networks

Record the forward pass. Replay it backward.

PyTorch builds neural networks using and replaying a tape recorder. Tensors live on the CPU or the GPU; every differentiable operation you run is recorded, and backward() plays the tape in reverse.

x = torch.randn(4, 3)
w = torch.randn(3, 2, requires_grad=True)
b = torch.zeros(2, requires_grad=True)

y = (x @ w + b).relu().sum()   # recorded as it runs
y.backward()                   # replayed in reverse
print(w.grad.shape)            # torch.Size([3, 2])
