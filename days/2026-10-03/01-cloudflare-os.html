Walk away.
Come back to a queue,
not a stall.

When an agent wants to take an action with side effects, a Gatekeeper simulates the outcome locally and lets it keep going. You approve or reject the queued actions later — in bulk, or one by one — when it is convenient for you, not when the agent hits step one.

Steps are illustrative; the mechanism is the project’s. If the agent reads back a result before you approve, the Gatekeeper answers with the simulated one.
