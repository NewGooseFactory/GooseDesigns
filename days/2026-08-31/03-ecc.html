It starts with the word "the"

Tuning the model is somebody else's job. This one tunes the harness.

Harness is the unusual noun

Most tooling in this space optimizes prompts or models. Putting the harness in the product name says the bottleneck is the scaffolding around the model.

Instincts is the odd part

Skills, memory, and security are familiar boxes. Instincts is the one that needs a definition, and the description does not give it one.

What is not on this page

No latency figure, no token saving, no before and after run, no definition of performance. The trending description publishes none of it.
