Your box is the server

The first sentence puts the whole stack on hardware you already own, which is the difference between a subscription and a machine you can unplug.

Seven capabilities is a wide promise

Inference, chat, voice, agents, workflows, RAG, and images are seven separate engineering problems. Naming them all in one line sets a high bar for the install.

What is not on this page

No model list, no hardware floor, no per-platform support table, no screenshot. The trending description publishes none of it.
