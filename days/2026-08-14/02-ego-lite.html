CITROLABS / EGO-LITE
Zero cost, zero config
One browser, two operators

Your tabs stay yours.

ego lite is a browser where you and your AI agents work in parallel. Agents run their browser tasks in their own Spaces while your tabs stay yours, and the agent still reaches your real logins through ego-browser.

The framework approach drives a second browser, so logins never carry cleanly and the two of you end up fighting over the same tab. Sharing one session store is the whole difference.

Automation framework

Two browsers, one fight

A framework needs a separate browser to drive. Logins do not carry cleanly, and you and the agent compete for the same tabs.

ego lite

One browser, two planes

The project describes a browser designed from the start for the two of you to share, with no extra setup.

Parallel

Tasks, not turns

Agents run several browser tasks at once in Spaces, so the work does not queue behind whatever you have open.

Logged in

The hard part is auth

Most browser automation dies at the login wall, which is why sharing real session state is the feature and not a detail.

Undisturbed

You keep working

The project states agents work without disturbing you, which is a claim about focus rather than throughput.
