Removing is the verb

The description says removes rather than rewrites, which sets the expectation that the tool subtracts and leaves your sentence rather than producing a new one in its own voice.

Signs is the plural that matters

A single tell can be an accident. A set of them together is what makes a page read as machine written, so the useful unit of work is the paragraph.
