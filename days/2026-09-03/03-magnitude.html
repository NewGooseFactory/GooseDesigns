Already use is the pitch

The value sits in requiring no change to your editor or agent. That framing puts the burden on the server to speak eight protocols rather than on you to switch tools.

Best is doing a lot of work

A server that picks a model for your hardware has to rank candidates on memory, speed, and quality. Publishing that ranking is what would turn best into a checkable word.

What is not on this page

No model names, no VRAM floor, no throughput, no selection rule. The trending description names eight clients and no models.
