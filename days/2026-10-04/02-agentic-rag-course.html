The project Week 7 Source

Learner-focused course · arXiv Paper Curator

Search first.
Vectors second.
Agents last.

Build a production RAG system one layer a week — a research assistant that fetches arXiv papers, understands them, and answers questions. Unlike tutorials that jump straight to vector search, the course masters keyword search foundations first, then enhances with vectors for hybrid retrieval.

Read the source Week 7 walkthrough

“Solid search foundations enhanced with AI, not AI-first approaches that ignore search fundamentals.”— the README’s own case for the order
