Long video is a
continuation problem.

LongCat-Video handles text-to-video, image-to-video and video-continuation in one model. Because it is natively pretrained on continuation, the project says it produces minutes-long videos without color drifting or quality degradation.

Why the long version stays affordable

three mechanisms the README names
01 · coarse to fine

Two axes, not one

Generation runs coarse-to-fine along both the temporal and the spatial axis, which is how 720p at 30 fps stays within minutes.

02 · block sparse attention

Cheaper at high resolution

Block Sparse Attention further improves efficiency, with the largest effect at high resolutions.

03 · multi-reward GRPO

Tuned against several rewards

Reinforcement learning from human feedback uses multi-reward Group Relative Policy Optimization rather than a single score.
