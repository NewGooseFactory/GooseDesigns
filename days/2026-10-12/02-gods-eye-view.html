The ceiling, stated

Terrestrial AIS goes quiet mid-ocean and satellite AIS costs real money; premium imagery, SAR and the deeper commercial feeds live behind enterprise contracts.

Not a limit of the architecture — every layer is a pattern you can point at your own data sources.

Before you share it

By default the server binds to localhost. Opt into LAN sharing and know this: a LAN-visible server brokers your configured API keys to anyone who can reach it.

App-level throttles are not billing caps — set provider quotas and budget alerts first.

Most feeds are live or regularly refreshed. Traffic is simulated along real roads using aggregate location data; CCTV camera poses and rocket launch trajectories are coarse estimates. — from the README
