The one red row

Google Skill Finder

It routes a request to the published Google skills that apply to it. The catalog is fetched on demand, so loading the finder costs almost nothing until a lookup actually happens — and its own description tells the agent to weigh when a Google option is not the right fit, and to skip entirely when the stack is already settled elsewhere. Read the skill.
