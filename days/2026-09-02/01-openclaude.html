Lowercase is the tone

The description is set in lowercase with two full stops and no adjectives. That reads as a claim made flatly rather than sold, which is a choice worth keeping in the type.

Anywhere is the harder half

Supporting many models is a matter of adapters. Running on any host touches packaging, permissions, and the filesystem, and it fails in ways users report as bugs.

What is not on this page
