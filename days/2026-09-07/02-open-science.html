Four hyphenated promises, and only reproducible can fail in public.

$ open-science
github.com/aipoch/open-science

Open-source, local-first, model-agnostic, cross-platform. All four are settled at install time by reading a licence, a config, or a download page. Reproducible provenance is the only one that has to keep being true months after the result was produced.

Every stage above happens once. Provenance is the claim that all four can be replayed later, including whichever choices the agents made along the way. That is the hardest promise in the sentence and the only one a reader cannot check on install day.

Agents in the chain raise the bar

A notebook replays deterministically. An agent making choices inside the pipeline has to record those choices too, or the replay produces a different answer.

Local-first and provenance fit together

If the whole workbench runs on your machine, the record of what happened lives there too, which removes the question of whether a service still holds it in a year.
