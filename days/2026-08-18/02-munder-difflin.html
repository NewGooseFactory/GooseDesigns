Word by word
local

The first word, and the one that decides who this is for. Nothing leaves the machine, so the agents you run are the ones you can inspect and the traces stay yours.

multi-agent

Plural, which means the hard part is coordination rather than capability. Anything calling itself multi-agent owes you an answer about who runs when and what they share.

harness

The load-bearing word. A framework asks you to build inside it; a harness wraps what you already have and holds it while it runs. That is a smaller promise and an easier one to keep.
