The tells it names out loud

project’s own anti-patterns
  • ×Overused fonts — Arial, Inter, system defaults
  • ×Purple-to-blue gradients
  • ×Cards nested inside cards
  • ×Gray text on colored backgrounds
  • ×Pure black or gray — always tint
  • ×The rounded-square icon tile above every heading
  • ×Bounce and elastic easing — feels dated

A named tell is a checkable tell. This list is why the detector rules can be deterministic at all — you cannot grep for “bad taste”, but you can grep for a nested card.

Hierarchy, clarity, emotional resonance — the judgments a rule cannot encode, kept in a separate tier rather than mixed into the rule count.

A shared vocabulary, in opposing pairs

bolderquieter distillpolishauditcritique shapecrafthardenanimate colorizetypesetlayoutdelight clarifyadapt

Shipping bolder and quieter as two commands is a quiet admission that there is no single correct amount of design — which is a more useful thing for a design tool to believe than a house style. 24 commands in total; the two highlighted are the pair that makes the point.
