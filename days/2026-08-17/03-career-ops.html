Runs locally in your AI coding CLI

A job search that grades the listing before you write the cover letter.

Scan the portals, score each listing against a structured rubric, tailor the CV, track what you sent. The whole loop stays inside the CLI you already have open.

Structured, not vibes

A letter grade that collapses to a number on a fixed 1.0 to 5.0 range is a thing you can sort. An impression is not.

Bring your own CLI

The README names Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and Antigravity. The tool assumes you already picked one.

Local by default

Your CV and your rejections stay on your machine. For this particular corpus that matters more than usual.
