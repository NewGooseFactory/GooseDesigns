What the sentence
commits to
Platform

A word that promises more than a library and less than an operating system. Using it means the pieces are meant to be adopted together, and that the seams between them are the vendor's problem rather than yours.

MAX

Named first inside the parenthesis. Naming a component in the one-line description is a decision to make it findable on its own, which is what you do when people already search for it by name.

Mojo

The repository's own language tag on the trending board, which makes it the only part of the sentence the page can verify from outside. Everything else here is the project describing itself.
