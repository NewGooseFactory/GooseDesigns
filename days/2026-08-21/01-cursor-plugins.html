Specification first

The spec is the product. The plugins are the proof.

Two things ship in one repository: a plugin specification, and the official plugins written against it. Publishing both is how you demonstrate that the format is implementable rather than aspirational.

Why ship both

A specification alone is a document. A specification with working implementations beside it is a format other people can trust enough to build on.

Official is a position

The word draws a line between what the vendor maintains and what the ecosystem writes. Both sit in one repository, which makes the line visible rather than implied.
