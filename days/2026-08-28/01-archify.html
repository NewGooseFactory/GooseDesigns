Why verifiable is the risk

A generated diagram that claims to be checkable invites someone to check it. That is a promise with a failure mode attached, which most output tools avoid making.

Export is the tell

Naming crisp export means the artifact leaves the tool. A diagram you can drop into a document outlives the session that produced it.
