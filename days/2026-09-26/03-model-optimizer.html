Model optimization library

Fixed ends.
Composable middle.

A library of state-of-the-art techniques — quantization, pruning, Neural Architecture Search, distillation, speculative decoding and sparsity. Python APIs compose them and export an optimized quantized checkpoint that downstream inference frameworks already load.

Why fix the ends

The formats you can bring and the runtimes you can ship to are enumerable. Publishing them is what makes the middle safe to rearrange.

Why keep the middle open

No single technique wins everywhere. Composition is the product — quantize, then prune, then distil, in whatever order the model tolerates.

What crosses the gap

A checkpoint, not a service. Nothing downstream has to change to accept an optimized model.
