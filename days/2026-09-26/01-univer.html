The Office harness for AI agents

One runtime.
People on one side,
agents on the other.

Univer is an open-source Office SDK for spreadsheets, documents and presentations. The same architecture runs UI apps in the browser and headless processing in Node.js, so people and AI agents can work in the same files.

    Output verification

    Agents check their own results through content inspection, rendered screenshots and layout diagnostics.

  • 03

    Worktree collaboration

    Agents work in isolated drafts; people review the changes and decide what to merge.
