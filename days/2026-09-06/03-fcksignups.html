A list defined entirely by its filter

Three conditions, and every one of them removes something.

Open-source, in-browser, no signups. None of the three describes what a tool does. All three describe what it does not ask of you, which makes the filter the product and the list itself the output.

A filter is easier to trust than a rating

Ranked lists need a scoring rule you have to accept. A pass or fail on three checkable conditions needs nothing from the reader except agreement that the conditions matter.

The three conditions compound

Any one of them alone is common. Together they describe a tool you can open, use once, and close, with no trace on either side of the transaction.
