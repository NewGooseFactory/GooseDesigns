Meta-harness is the interesting noun

A harness runs one agent. A meta-harness coordinates harnesses, which is a layer above where most of this board sits and a harder thing to keep stable.

Six capabilities is a wide surface

Swarms, workflows, memory, learning, retrieval, and conversation are six separate systems. Naming all of them in one line sets a high bar for the install.
