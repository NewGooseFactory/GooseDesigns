macro-inc / macro Unified workspace

Shared AI memory

Seven tools, one thing that remembers.

macro is a unified workspace for teams covering email, chat, docs, tasks, agents, calls, and CRM, at-linked together with shared AI memory. The link and the memory are what make it one workspace instead of seven tabs.

01

The link is the schema

An at-mention that resolves the same way everywhere is a lightweight way to give a team one shared vocabulary.

02

Shared, not per-user

Memory that belongs to the team means the second person to ask gets what the first person already established.

03

Agents are one surface

Listing agents alongside email and calls treats them as another participant rather than a separate product.
