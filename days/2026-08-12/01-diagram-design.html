Six plates drawn illustratively. The project states 29 editorial diagram types, self-contained HTML and SVG, with no shadows and no Mermaid-slop.

01

Editorial, not generated

A diagram type carries a point of view about what the reader should compare. Rendering a graph is the easy half.

02

Self-contained output

HTML and SVG with nothing to install means the artifact travels into a doc, a PR, or a page without a build step.

03

The constraints are the product

No shadows and no Mermaid-slop are design rules stated up front, which tells you what the author is refusing.
